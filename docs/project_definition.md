### 📌 Project Definition: Customer Churn Prediction System

#### 1\. What is Churn?



Churn means a customer stops using the company’s service.

For example:



* A customer cancels their phone connection
* A Netflix subscriber stops renewing
* A person closes their bank account



In this project, churn is a binary label:



* 1 → Customer has churned
* 0 → Customer is active



#### 2\. Why Do Customers Churn?



Customers leave companies for many reasons:



###### Business Factors

* High prices
* Better offers from competitors
* Poor service or support
* Technical issues
* Billing problems



###### Customer Behavior Factors

* Low engagement
* Irregular payments
* Short tenure (new customers churn more)
* Less usage of services



###### External Factors

* Customer moved to a new location
* Personal financial issues
* Lifestyle changes



Understanding these patterns helps a company avoid future loss.



#### 3\. Business Impact of Churn



Churn causes financial and strategic loss:



###### Direct Loss



When a customer leaves, the company loses:

* Monthly subscription fees
* Future revenue
* Cross-selling opportunities



Example:

If one customer pays ₹600/month → yearly loss = ₹600 × 12 = ₹7,200



###### Indirect Loss

* Cost of acquiring a new customer (marketing, ads)
* Negative word of mouth
* Lower market reputation



📌 Keeping existing customers is 5–7 times cheaper than acquiring new ones.



#### 4\. How Will Machine Learning Help?



A machine learning model can:



###### 1\. Predict which customers are likely to churn

It gives a risk score (0–100%) for each customer.



###### 2\. Provide early warning signals

The business can take action before the customer leaves.



###### 3\. Give reasons WHY a customer might churn

Using SHAP, we can explain:

* High monthly charges
* Short tenure
* No tech support
* Month-to-month contract



###### 4\. Help business teams focus on high-risk customers

Marketing can:

* Give discounts
* Offer personalized plans
* Call high-risk customers
* Improve service



###### ⭐ Final Benefit:



ML helps increase customer retention, reduce revenue loss, and improve customer satisfaction.



#### 5\. Project Objective (One Line)



Build an end-to-end machine learning system that predicts customer churn, explains the reasons behind churn, and provides actionable insights to improve retention strategies.



#### 6\. Target Users (Stakeholders)



* Marketing team – to run retention campaigns

* Product managers – to understand service issues
* Business leaders – to track churn KPIs
* Recruiters/Hiring managers – to evaluate technical skills
